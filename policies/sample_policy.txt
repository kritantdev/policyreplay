ACME STORE OPERATIONS POLICY (v1)

1. Refunds (P-01). Refunds above INR 10,000 require manager approval. Support staff may issue smaller refunds directly.
2. Roles (P-02). Only administrators can modify user roles.
3. Orders (P-03). Shipped orders cannot be cancelled.
4. Discounts (P-04). Discounts cannot exceed 30%.
5. Privacy (P-05). Customers cannot access another customer's order.
